# Use of AI tools

This statement follows the AAMAS 2027 policy on AI-assisted technologies. No AI tool is an author of the paper.

The authors used AI tools to write and revise the text of the paper, to help with coding (including the experiment
scripts and running experiments), and to brainstorm ideas and build prototypes. The authors reviewed all
AI-generated content and code, decided what to keep, and take full responsibility for the paper and the supplementary
material.
